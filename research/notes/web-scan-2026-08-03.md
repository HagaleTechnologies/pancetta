## Noted but not bank-worthy

### arXiv:2607.20130 — Quantum LDPC Cluster-Based Sequential BP (July 22, 2026)

(Carried from July 27 scan; confirmed inapplicable.) RL agent selects
variable-node clusters to update at each BP scheduling step for quantum LDPC
codes. Quantum-only (stabilizer formalism, degenerate errors); no classical
application. The late-July arXiv 2607.21000–2607.25000+ range was dominated
by a quantum LDPC cluster consistent with the pattern noted in prior scans;
no classical short-block papers found in this range.

### arXiv:2603.07526 — Finite Blocklength ORBGRAND Analysis (March 2026)

**Technique**: Theoretical analysis of ORBGRAND's word error rate in the
finite blocklength regime for LDPC-CRC concatenated codes. Provides
closed-form bounds on the expected number of queries needed as a function of
blocklength, redundancy, and SNR.

**Why not bank-worthy**: This paper is theoretical analysis supporting the
architecture already captured in hb-267 (Two-Stage BP+GRAND-on-CRC, Kong et
al. Entropy 2025 + TechRxiv Feb 2026). The finite-BL analysis does not
introduce a new algorithmic technique — it provides bounds that inform the
query-budget parameterization already in hb-267's probe plan (Q ∈ {50, 200,
500, 2000}). No separate bank entry needed; the practical implementation probe
is hb-267.

### arXiv:2604.07004 — LDPC Decoding under Bursty Phase Noise (April 2026)

**Technique**: Iterative LDPC decoding with a phase-noise-aware LLR
computation for optical fiber channels with Wiener phase noise (laser
linewidth-induced bursty phase rotation). Modified sum-product with
phase-tracking auxiliary variables.

**Why not bank-worthy**: Target channel is coherent optical fiber (phase-locked
laser carrier, Wiener-process phase noise). FT8 operates over HF radio with
8-GFSK modulation — differential encoding removes carrier phase entirely, and
the channel model (multipath, ionospheric Doppler, additive Gaussian +
impulsive noise) has no Wiener phase noise component. The LLR modification is
specific to the optical coherent channel model. Not transferable.

### arXiv:2601.06732 — AR-CID Adaptive Residual BP (January 2026)

(Carried from July 6 scan; dead-ended in that scan.) AR-CID uses residual
message updates and adaptive scheduling, measuring best for medium-to-long
codes. For FT8's 174-bit LDPC code, graph density is too high for the
adaptive scheduling to converge to a gain. No bank entry; confirmed dead-ended.

### arXiv:2604.06889 — aSCED Affine Subcode Ensemble Decoding (April 2026)

variant of SCED — is already explicitly noted in hb-262 as a covering entry.
