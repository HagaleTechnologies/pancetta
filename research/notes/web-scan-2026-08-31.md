## Noted but not bank-worthy

### WSJT-X Improved v3.2.0 — clarification of decoder changes (not bank-worthy)

Full release notes obtained (111 kB, Release_Notes.txt dated 2026-08-15;
installer binaries 2026-08-17–2026-08-20). Key FT8-related changes:

**"a8" decoding technology (MTD)**:
- MTD 3-Stage "now (partially) supports the new 'a8' decoding technology,"
  enabling display of messages "0.5 to 1 second earlier."
- "a8" is an AP level, not a new LDPC algorithm: it encodes the use of
  "certain information from previous Rx sequences." The release notes
  group MTD AP types a31/a35 as "a9", and "a8" is an intermediate level
  in the AP hierarchy (above a7, below a9).
- Cross-TR sequence information is the mechanism. This is the direction
  that pancetta's hb-237 (cross-sequence AP), hb-085 (cross-cycle residual),
  and hb-173 (cross-time callsign exploitation) explored. hb-237 was measured
  negative in two independent pancetta probes: Batch 54 (+0 TPs / +796 FPs)
  and Batch 67 (+0 TPs / +1233 FPs on real data). WSJT-X Improved's a8
  result may reflect a different operating corpus or trust model.

**"Optimized baseline calculation" (STD)**:
- "Optimized baseline calculation, effective for FT4, FT2 and FT8 (STD)" with
  automatic optimization of filter edges based on actual transceiver passband.
- This is a filter/passband change, not an LDPC algorithm change. Reduces
  false decodes when Wide Graph limits are poorly set.

**Conclusion**: The previous scan (2026-08-24) concluded "no FT8 LDPC algorithm
changes" from the release notes. After direct fetch, this is confirmed: no changes
to LDPC, BP schedule, OSD, or LLR computation. The a8 AP level and baseline
optimization are operationally significant but architecturally distinct from the
LDPC decoder. Not bank-worthy; documented here to close the gap.

**Status update**: WSJT-X Improved is confirmed at v3.2.0 (2026-08-15–20).
